---
title: How to use it
status: published
---

1. **Define the intended action and objective.** What is the system for, and what is it allowed to touch?
2. **Assess the autonomy level (L0–L5).** Not what the technology could do — what this deployment is permitted to do.
3. **Assess the impact level (I0–I3).** Ask what happens when it is wrong, not when it is right.
4. **Apply the control posture at the intersection before deployment.**

The posture is a floor, not a ceiling. Nothing prevents an organisation applying stronger controls than the cell requires, and the escalation triggers in each cell describe when it should.
