In November 2022 an airline's website chatbot told a grieving passenger they could claim a bereavement fare after travelling. The information was wrong, and a tribunal later required the airline to compensate the passenger. That chatbot could not reach a single external system. Responsibility did not begin when the system was allowed to act — it was already there.

Agentic systems call tools, write to external systems and take actions that were never individually approved. The governance question moves from what a model might say to what an agent can actually do. Published guidance names agentic risk and tells organisations to assess it; none of it says how much control a given deployment needs. This project fills that gap.

**A governance instrument, not an assurance certificate.** The scoring rules were defined inside the project and validated internally across eight scenarios, not by an independent assessor. Version one addresses single-agent deployments only.
