---
title: Limitations
status: published
---

**This is a governance instrument, not an assurance certificate.** Applying the matrix does not make a system safe, and no cell should be read as a claim that the listed controls are sufficient in a particular deployment.

**Ratings involve judgement.** Where members of the project team disagreed on a cell, the published rating is the one we could evidence, and the disagreement is noted rather than smoothed over.

**Evidence has limits, and we have tried to state them.** Where a control's efficacy rests on a study, we have recorded what that study did not test. A control that has never been tested in the conditions you plan to deploy it in is a hypothesis.

**Columns are at different stages.** Sections marked as drafts are not yet reviewed and are hidden from this page.
