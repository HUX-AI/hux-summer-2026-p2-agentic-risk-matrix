Each column was researched by a different member of the project team against a shared reference scale, then reconciled. Cells cite the evidence that justifies their rating, and that evidence is listed in full in the reference tracker below.

Two conventions are worth stating because they are easy to get wrong:

**Impact is a property of the deployment, not the technology.** The same retrieval chatbot is negligible-impact on a canteen menu and critical-impact on a safeguarding pathway. Classify the use case.

**Colour means risk and nothing else.** No colour appears anywhere on this page except in the matrix and its legend.
